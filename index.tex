\titre{Research interest}
\label{sec:org0f47217}
\vspace{.5cm}

  My research combines \textbf{economic theory} and \textbf{empirical
methods} to generate evidence that supports \textbf{better private
decisions} and \textbf{public policies}. I specialize in \textbf{wine
economics} and enjoy collaborating across disciplines to
address \textbf{agricultural}, \textbf{environmental}, and \textbf{urban}
outcomes.
